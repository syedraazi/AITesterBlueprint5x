# STAR: Interview or Resume Answer

**Use when:** You need to describe a project you tested as an interview answer or a resume bullet.

| Part | Content |
|------|---------|
| **S**ituation | Project {{project_name}}, domain {{domain}}, team size {{team_size}} |
| **T**ask | Your responsibility: {{your_role}} |
| **A**ction | What you did: {{actions_taken}} |
| **R**esult | Measurable outcome: {{outcome}} |

## Copy-paste prompt
```
Situation: I worked on {{project_name}} in the {{domain}} domain with a team of {{team_size}}.
Task: My responsibility was {{your_role}}.
Action: I did the following: {{actions_taken}}.
Result: The outcome was {{outcome}}.
Turn this into (a) a 60-second spoken interview answer and (b) one resume bullet.
Use only the facts I provided. Do not add numbers, tools, or claims I did not give you.
```
