# CLEAR: Status Email

**Use when:** You need a short, clean status email to a mixed audience.

| Part | Content |
|------|---------|
| **C**ontext | Project {{project_name}}, period {{date_or_range}}, audience {{audience}}, notes {{raw_notes}} (source: <url>) |
| **L**imit | Under {{word_limit}} words, no jargon, no invented facts |
| **E**xample | {{sample_status_email}} |
| **A**ction | Write the email in the order Progress, Issues, Next Steps with dates and owners |
| **R**eview | Re-check numbers and dates, list anything unclear or missing |

## Copy-paste prompt
```
Context: Project {{project_name}}, period {{date_or_range}}. The audience is {{audience}}. My notes: {{raw_notes}} (source: <url>).
Limit: Under {{word_limit}} words. Professional wording, no jargon. Do not invent facts or numbers. Use "TBD" if something is missing.
Example: Follow the style of this email: {{sample_status_email}}
Action: Write a status email with a subject line, in the order Progress, Issues, Next Steps. Include dates and owners.
Review: After the email, check that every number and date comes from my notes, and list anything unclear or missing.
```
