# STAR: Blocker Escalation Email

**Use when:** A blocker is delaying testing and you need a manager to act.

| Part | Content |
|------|---------|
| **S**ituation | {{blocker}} has delayed testing by {{days}} days on {{project_name}} |
| **T**ask | Write an escalation email to {{recipient}} |
| **A**ction | State the impact on schedule and scope, and what has already been tried |
| **R**esult | End with a clear ask and a needed-by date |

## Copy-paste prompt
```
Situation: {{blocker}} has delayed testing by {{days}} days on {{project_name}}. Ticket: <url>.
Task: Write an escalation email to {{recipient}}.
Action: State the impact on schedule and scope, and what has already been tried to resolve it.
Result: End with one clear ask and a needed-by date of {{needed_by_date}}.
Tone: professional and factual, not emotional. Include a subject line. Under 150 words.
```
